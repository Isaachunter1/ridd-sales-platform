# Design taste — what Cameron has said yes and no to


## The Riddmarket look is signed (2026-09-15)

Off the live /market/login — white ground, wordmark top left, RIDDMARKET in
mono top right, mono labels over hairline inputs, a full-width black LOGIN
plate: "YES BTW on this look and feel. this is GOLD." Direction A (queue

market surface.

## Utilities are chrome, never destinations (2026-09-17)

What went wrong: the public MENU's five Anton rows are the site's destinations
for a recruit — APPLY NOW first. The desk, wiring one sign-in (queue Q-0311),
wrote "a LOGIN row" in its own context line, and the hands built exactly that:
LOGIN as a sixth numbered headline row. Cameron: "the login should not be that
huge in a navigation bar wtf? … that's completely irregular and distasteful."

The rule, so the class does not recur:

- **Destinations are big; utilities are small.** Login, account, cart, search,
  sign out, language, theme — these are chrome. They sit in the bar or as a
  small mono line, never as peers of the headline rows or rail items, on any
  surface (public menu, market, deck, slicks).
- **The desk does not specify placement.** The desk states the function and
  the constraint ("a way to sign in from the menu; it is a utility, not a
  destination"). Where it goes is the hands' proposal, shown as a capture
  before it ships, on any surface he has signed (public nav, market, doors).
- **Anything added to a signed surface is a taste change.** The market look
  (Q-0273), the doors (Q-0275) and the public menu are signed. Adding an
  element to them gets a capture first, every time, no matter how small the
  ask sounds.
